About the Museum

Where Learning Meets the Sea

Founded in 2003, Museo Sang Bata sa Negros is an alternative learning sanctuary dedicated to awakening children's creative and intellectual potential through hands-on marine conservation education and cultural discovery.

📍

Coastal Location

Situated on the shoreline of Barangay Old Sagay, Sagay City, Negros Occidental, right beside the 32,000-hectare protected Sagay Marine Reserve.

🕒

Operating Hours

Open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
Saturday and Sunday visits are available for booked educational group tours.

🎟️

Admission & Rates

Regular Tour: ₱20.00 (Students w/ ID), ₱50.00 (Adults), ₱40.00 (Seniors).
Guided Tours led by Junior Guides available from ₱130 to ₱800.

🌏

Global Network

Proud member of the SAMP Intercontinental Museum Network and the West Visayan Association of Museums, bridging local communities with international best practices.

Learning & Outreach

Signature Educational Programs

Beyond physical exhibits, MSBN pioneers community-driven programs that empower youth leadership, environmental literacy, and cultural preservation.

🧒

Junior Museum Guide Program

Local youth aged 8–12 are trained in public speaking and marine science to serve as docents for museum visitors. Over 300 children have graduated.

📖

Junior Interactive Storytelling

Children interpret marine stories and folk tales through song, dance, and creative drama, fostering literacy in the Museo Reading Club.

🌱

4 Vegetable Gardening Program

A hands-on organic gardening project where young learners cultivate nutritious vegetables, promoting community food security and soil health.

🐠

Marine Conservation Education (MCEP)

Our flagship initiative training public elementary science teachers, fisherfolk, and coastal mothers in marine biodiversity protection and climate resilience.
